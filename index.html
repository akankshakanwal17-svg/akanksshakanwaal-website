The Journey

Ten sarees to a thousand founders

The story — and the mission it became.

2015

Ten sarees and a Facebook page

With just ₹5,000, she bought ten handloom sarees from a weaver in Phulia, opened a free Facebook page, and started selling. No capital, no plan B — just a belief that India would buy handloom online.

2017

The customers came back

Again and again. The repeat-customer model in online handloom was working — and a side hustle quietly became a real business.

2020

The hardest year

When the lockdown left weavers without work, she kept buying — keeping looms running and families paid. The worst year and the most defining one, at once.

2022

From a page to a brand

Beatitude had become the real thing — a team, a supply chain, and weavers with steady year-round work. All of it bootstrapped, every step on her own terms.

2024

The exit

She stepped away from Beatitude as its Director — the ₹50 crore brand she had built from a ₹5,000 start now standing fully on its own. One chapter closed, on purpose.

2025

The next level

The vision sharpens: take good brands to where they can't yet see. Already 1,000+ students and 100 startups mentored, pharma brands guided past a combined ₹1,000 crore, and government projects delivered — now channelled into helping established brands grow and scale organically.

"You don't need a big budget. You need passion — and the consistency to back it."
— Akankssha Kanwaal
